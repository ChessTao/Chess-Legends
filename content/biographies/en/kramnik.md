# Vladimir Kramnik

Vladimir Kramnik (born in 1975) is a Russian chess grandmaster, the fourteenth World Chess Champion (2000-2007).

## Main chess achievements

In January 1996, Kramnik became the youngest ever to reach world number one; this record would stand for 14 years until being broken by Magnus Carlsen in January 2010.

In March 2000, Kasparov announced he would play a match against Kramnik. Kramnik defeated his rival in London in 2000 with a score of 8.5–6.5 without losing a game and became the fourteenth World Chess Champion.

In 2004, Kramnik defended his title against challenger Peter Leko with a score of 7-7.

FIDE organized a reunification match between Kramnik and Veselin Topalov in 2006. Kramnik won it on the tie-break by a score of 2.5–1.5.

The next World Championship match was held in 2008 in Bonn, Germany, between Kramnik and Anand. Kramnik lost it by a score of 6.5-4.5.

Kramnik is the winner of the Chess World Cup 2013.

He won three team gold medals and three individual medals at Chess Olympiads as a member of the Russian team.

## Legacy

Kramnik was a player comfortable in any opening and, hence, any position. No opening is more closely associated with Kramnik than the Berlin Defense to the Ruy Lopez, thanks to his use of it to neutralize Kasparov’s use of the white pieces in 2000.
