# Ian Nepomniachtchi

Ian Nepomniachtchi (born in 1990) is a Russian chess grandmaster, one of the world's strongest players from the early 2010s until the present time.

## Main chess achievements

In 2010, Nepomniachtchi won the European Individual Championship. Later the same year, he won the Russian Chess Championship. In October 2013, he tied for first with Peter Svidler in the Russian Championship Superfinal, finishing second on tiebreak.

In the FIDE Grand Prix 2019 series, Nepomniachtchi won two out of three tournaments in which he played. He finished second in the overall standings, qualifying him for the 2020 Candidates’ Tournament. In December 2020, he again won the Russian championship.

A win in the 2020/2021 Candidates’ tournament qualified Nepomniachtchi to challenge Magnus Carlsen in a match for the World Chess Championship in November–December 2021. Carlsen retained his title, winning 7.5–3.5.

Nepomniachtchi qualified for the 2022 Candidates’ tournament as the World Championship runner-up and took an early lead. He won by getting the highest score of 9.5 out of 14 in any Candidates' tournament since the modern format was introduced in 2013.

In his second FIDE World Chess Championship 2023, Nepomniachtchi had a tied score of 7–7 with Ding Liren in the classical portion of the match but ended up losing 2.5–1.5 in the tiebreak playoff.

Nepomniachtchi won numerous prestigious chess tournaments: Aeroflot Open (2006 and 2015), Danzhou (2016), Tal Memorial (2016), London Chess Classic (2017), Dortmund (2018), Sinquefield Cup 2022 (Saint Louis), WR Chess Masters 2023 (Dusseldorf) and others.

Nepomniachtchi is exceptionally strong in fast time controls; however, he has struggled for a long time to secure the World Blitz or Rapid title. He finished second at the World Blitz Championship in 2014 and earned three medals in the World Rapid Championships: a bronze in 2017 and 2024 and a silver in 2021. Finally, on December 31, 2024, he became a co-winner (with Magnus Carlsen) of the World Blitz Chess Championship.

## Legacy

Together with Vasily Smyslov, Nepomniachtchi is the only one who managed to win two Candidates' tournaments.
